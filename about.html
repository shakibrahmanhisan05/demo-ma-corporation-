Since 1985

Forty years of clearing cargo at Chattogram.

A team of experienced customs professionals in Agrabad, a short drive from the port that carries most of Bangladesh’s trade.

Our story

Built on the Custom House floor.

M. A. Trading Corporation Agencies Ltd. started in 1985 as a customs clearing & forwarding agent in Agrabad, Chattogram’s commercial district. Our job then is our job now: get our clients’ cargo through customs correctly, quickly and without surprises.

As clients asked us to take care of what happened after release, we set up Alliance Transport International to run our own road transport — and opened a Dhaka office to stay close to clients in the capital.

Milestones

Forty years, step by step.

From paper files to ASYCUDA World, the tools have changed. The commitment to doing it right hasn’t.

  1. 1985

    Founded in Agrabad

    M. A. Trading Corporation Agencies starts as a customs C&F agent in Chattogram’s commercial heart, close to the port.

  2. Growth

    Trusted by importers & exporters

    Long-standing relationships with importers, exporters, shipping lines and the Custom House are built one shipment at a time.

  3. Expansion

    Alliance Transport International

    A sister concern is set up to run our own road transport, so clearance and delivery are handled by one group.

  4. Dhaka

    A Dhaka office

    Opened to stay close to clients in the capital and coordinate shipments there.

  5. 2025

    40 years

    Four decades of clearing cargo through Chattogram.

  6. Today

    Going digital

    Online filing on ASYCUDA World, digital document sharing — and a new website for clients and overseas principals.

What we stand for

How we work

Integrity

Correct declarations, honest advice and every charge backed by a receipt. Our reputation at the Custom House took forty years to build.

Speed

Documents checked before arrival, people on the ground at the port, and trucks planned ahead. Time at the port is money.

Transparency

You always know which stage your cargo is at, and what it is costing — duties, port charges and our fee shown separately.

Care

We handle every consignment as if it were our own — from a carton of samples to a factory’s new production line.
