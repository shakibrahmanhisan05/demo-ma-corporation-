The process

How your import clears — step by step

Six stages from the ship’s manifest to your factory gate. We manage every one of them, and you always know which stage your cargo is at.

01 Step 01 of 06

Pre-arrival check

Send us your shipping documents as soon as the vessel or flight is booked. We check the paperwork, confirm the HS code and estimate duties before your cargo arrives — so there are no surprises at the Custom House.

What we need from you

  • Commercial invoice & packing list
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill
  • LC / LCAF copy, IRC, e-TIN & BIN

What we handle

  • HS code classification
  • Duty & tax estimate
  • Permit and certificate check
02 Step 02 of 06

Bill of Entry

Once the vessel’s manifest (IGM) is filed, we lodge your Bill of Entry on ASYCUDA World — Bangladesh Customs’ online system — with every supporting document attached.

What we need from you

  • Signed authorisation letter
  • Insurance cover note
  • Certificate of origin (if claiming benefits)

What we handle

  • Online declaration on ASYCUDA World
  • Supporting documents submitted
  • Queries answered at the Custom House
03 Step 03 of 06

Assessment & duty

Customs assesses the declaration and calculates duties and taxes. We review the assessment, settle any queries with the appraisers and arrange payment through your bank so the release isn’t held up.

What we need from you

  • Duty funds through your bank or pay order

What we handle

  • Assessment review
  • Query resolution with appraisers
  • Payment and receipts
04 Step 04 of 06

Examination

If your consignment is selected for physical inspection, our people attend the examination at the port alongside customs officers — and make sure your goods are handled with care.

What we need from you

  • Nothing extra — our team is on site

What we handle

  • Examination attendance
  • Sampling & lab tests if required
  • Follow-up on the examination report
05 Step 05 of 06

Port release

We collect the shipping line’s Delivery Order, settle port and storage charges, and obtain the release order and gate pass. Every hour saved here is demurrage you don’t pay.

What we need from you

  • Approval of port & shipping-line charges

What we handle

  • Delivery Order (DO) collection
  • Port & storage bills
  • Gate pass and exit
06 Step 06 of 06

Door delivery

Our sister concern, Alliance Transport International, loads your cargo at the port and delivers it to your warehouse or factory — then returns the empty container to the depot.

What we need from you

  • Delivery address & unloading contact

What we handle

  • Truck & trailer dispatch
  • Delivery anywhere in Bangladesh
  • Empty container return
